01The problem

A three-hour nightly stream on a small channel has three enemies. Lurkers who watch but never chat. Viewers who leave at the ad break and do not come back. Followers who visit once and never return.

Chat games existed, but none of them rewarded the four behaviours my channel needed in one coherent loop: staying, chatting, returning and subscribing. Most were a single command with a random result and no progression, so they were fun for a week and then ignored.

The constraints were just as clear. No budget, no game engine, one person. It had to run live six nights a week without ever breaking the stream, and a new viewer had to be able to start playing from chat with no explanation.

02The design

Everything in Stream Dex hangs off one loop. A wild Pokémon appears on the stream overlay, you throw a ball from chat, you collect copies, you evolve them live with sound and animation, and you show the result off: your Buddy walks next to your name in chat and your Pokédex page fills up. Then you want the next one.

Core loop

Two commands carry the whole loop. Everything else below exists to give players a reason to come back to it.

Progression layers

Each layer adds one more reason to return. None of them is required to play; all of them reward presence.

LayerMechanicBehaviour it rewards
CollectionWild spawns at random while I stream. One throw per spawn. Tiered balls: Poké (free, never runs out), Great, Ultra, Master (guaranteed).Being present when a spawn happens.
EvolutionThree copies evolve one Pokémon (Magikarp and Swablu need 20, Feebas 10, Nincada 9). Some lines also need a stone, a 2,500-point friendship bond, or a trade. Wurmple and Clamperl evolve at random.Long-term collecting, planning, social play.
BuddyEquip any Pokémon you own. It appears beside your chat name and friendship grows while you watch. !buddy greet banks it, !buddy pet adds 10 with a Pet Token, !buddy feed adds 250 with a Berry. Friendship is shared across the evolution line.Watch time, chat identity, small daily rituals.
EggsOne Egg at a time. It hatches after 20 hours in the stream, and the time only counts while you are there. Baby Pokémon never appear in the wild.Return visits across many streams.
TradingPlayer-to-player offers in chat with a one-minute window to answer and a repeat to confirm. Trade evolutions fire automatically when both sides end up with three or more.Viewer-to-viewer relationships.
Pokédex siteA personal page per player: regions, type mastery, catch performance, Trainer Journey, Buddy, bag and shop. !pokedex rebuilds it and posts the link in chat.Pride, completion drive, a shareable link.

Economy: faucets and sinks

Pokecoins are the only currency. Every number below is live today and documented in the Trainer's Handbook.

Faucets (coins in)

  • Coins trickle in while you watch. Subscribers earn a little extra.
  • Every catch pays. A Legendary catch pays everyone in chat.
  • Every ad break pays everyone 150 coins, then a 90-second !bonus window opens: 100 unsubscribed, 350 Tier 1, 600 Tier 2, 850 Tier 3.
  • Release spare fully evolved Pokémon for coins, up to 10,000 for a Legendary. Stone evolutions pay extra. You always keep one copy.
  • Predictions, challenges and giveaways on stream.

Guardrails

  • Bag caps on every item, so hoarding cannot break the economy.
  • You never pay for something you cannot carry: a full bag means nothing is charged.
  • Mythic Pokémon (Mew, Celebi, Jirachi, Deoxys) cannot be traded or released and never spawn in the wild.
  • One throw per spawn, one trade at a time, one Egg at a time, one bonus claim per ad break.
  • An evolution waits until the encounter on screen has finished.

Social broadcast moments

  • A Master Ball purchase is announced to the whole stream, on purpose.
  • A Legendary catch pays everyone in chat, so one player's luck becomes a shared moment.
  • Evolutions and hatches play on the overlay with sound, so the room reacts.

Retention hooks mapped to Twitch's real problems

Twitch problemWhat Stream Dex does about it
Ad-break drop-off150 coins to everyone who is still there when the ad ends, then a 90-second bonus window. Leaving at the ad break costs you twice.
LurkersCoins and Buddy friendship accrue silently while you watch, so lurking is still progress. The first catch is one word in chat.
Followers who never returnEggs and friendship only advance while you are present. A 20-hour Egg is six or seven streams.
MonetisationSubscribers earn more per hour and the ad-break bonus scales by tier (100, 350, 600, 850). Gifted subs, bits and predictions all feed back into the game.

03The build

There is no game engine. The bot does the game logic, a Python script turns each player's data into a JSON file, and a static website on GitHub Pages renders it. A watchdog pushes the data after every change, so a player's page is live a few seconds after they type !pokedex.

Stack, stated honestly

  • Mix It Up (stream bot, no-code logic): every command, timer, currency rule, overlay event and sound.
  • Python: the pipeline that reads the bot's variables and the Pokémon list, computes stats, evolution requirements and type matchups, and writes one JSON file per player. A second script watches the data folder and commits and pushes on every change.
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON: the Pokédex site, fully client-side, on GitHub Pages.
  • AI-assisted coding: I used AI tools while writing the Python and the website code. The design, the rules, the numbers and every decision about what to build are mine.

I am the only person who has worked on it. The public repository holds 896 commits as of 2 November 2026: about 800 of them are the watchdog pushing player data after streams, and 93 are my own changes to the site.
